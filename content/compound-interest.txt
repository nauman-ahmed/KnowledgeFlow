Compound Interest (Plain Language)

Simple interest grows only on the original amount (principal).

Compound interest grows on the principal plus interest already earned. Each period, you earn interest on a larger base.

Example: $1,000 at 5% annual compound interest for 3 years:
- Year 1: $1,050
- Year 2: $1,102.50
- Year 3: $1,157.63

The Rule of 72 estimates doubling time: divide 72 by the annual rate (%). At 6%, money roughly doubles in 12 years.

Start early; time in the market matters as much as the rate of return.
