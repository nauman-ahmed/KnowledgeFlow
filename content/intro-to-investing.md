# Introduction to Investing

Investing means putting money to work so it can grow over time. Common vehicles include stocks, bonds, and index funds.

## Key ideas

- **Compound interest** — Earnings on your earnings; small regular contributions add up over decades.
- **Diversification** — Spreading money across assets reduces risk from any single failure.
- **Time horizon** — Longer horizons can tolerate more volatility; short-term goals favor safer assets.
- **Fees** — Expense ratios and trading costs eat returns; low-cost index funds are popular for beginners.

## Risk

All investments carry risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Match your strategy to your goals and tolerance for loss.
