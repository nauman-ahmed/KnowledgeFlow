Music Theory Basics

A scale is a sequence of notes ordered by pitch. The major scale follows the pattern:
whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half (W-W-H-W-W-W-H).

A chord is three or more notes played together. A major triad contains the 1st, 3rd, and 5th
degrees of a major scale (root, major third, perfect fifth).

Rhythm divides time into beats. Time signatures like 4/4 mean four quarter-note beats per bar.
Tempo is measured in BPM (beats per minute).

Harmony describes how chords relate. Common progressions in pop music include I–V–vi–IV
(for example C–G–Am–F in the key of C major).
