# Effective Study Habits

Spaced repetition beats cramming. Review material at increasing intervals (1 day, 3 days, 1 week) to move facts into long-term memory.

## Active recall

Close the book and explain the concept aloud or on paper. Testing yourself strengthens memory more than re-reading highlights.

## Environment

- One dedicated study block (45–90 minutes) with phone away
- Same place when possible to cue focus
- Short breaks every hour (5–10 minutes)

## Sleep

Memory consolidation happens during sleep. All-nighters hurt retention more than they help.
